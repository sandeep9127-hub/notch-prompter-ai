Your words sit a few millimetres from the lens, so you look at people, not at notes.

Follow my voice

It scrolls when you speak, and waits when you don't.

Apple's on-device speech recognition tracks where you are. Words you've said fade out, a green glow pulses with your voice, and if you pause or ad-lib, the script waits for you.

Prefer a steady pace? Auto-scroll at any speed, with a 3-2-1 countdown to get set.

Live call cues

Stuck on a question? Get a nudge.

Turn on cues and NotchCoach listens to the other side of the call. When they pause, up to three short replies appear under your script, based on your own notes.

With Gemma or Apple Intelligence, it all happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

Always in control

One button stops everything.

The red Stop button (or ⌃⌥Esc) instantly turns off scrolling, the microphone, call audio and any AI request, and confirms it on screen. The menu bar icon turns red whenever anything is listening.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Download

Get NotchCoach.dmg and drag NotchCoach into Applications.

Allow it to open

NotchCoach isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks first. Open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/NotchCoach.app

Paste your script

Click the logo in the menu bar, paste your script, and press play. Allow the microphone when you first use Follow my voice.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. NotchCoach is free and open source under the MIT licence. Gemma and Apple Intelligence are free too. Only Claude costs money, billed by Anthropic to your own API key, and only if you choose it.

Can people on the call see it?

NotchCoach asks macOS to keep the prompter out of screen sharing and recordings. Meeting apps capture the screen in different ways, so test it once with your app by sharing your screen before an important call.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Apple shows that warning for apps that aren't distributed through the App Store or notarised with a paid developer account. The full source code is public on GitHub, so you can check exactly what it does, or build it yourself.

Does it record me?

No. Speech is transcribed live, on your Mac when your language supports it, and isn't saved. Live cues send the transcript to an AI only if you choose Claude; with Gemma or Apple Intelligence, it stays on your Mac. If you use live cues, tell the people on the call.

Does it work on Intel Macs or Macs without a notch?

It needs an Apple-silicon Mac (M1 or newer). On a Mac or display without a notch, use the floating window or second-display mode.

Which languages does Follow my voice support?

English (India, US and UK) and Hindi to start, through Apple's speech recognition. More can be added; open an issue on GitHub.

Something isn't working. What should I do?

Click Stop everything first. Then open the menu bar icon → Open Log File and attach it to a bug report on GitHub.
